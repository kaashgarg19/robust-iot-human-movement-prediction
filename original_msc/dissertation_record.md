# MSc Dissertation Record

**Original title:** Intelligent System to Predict Human Movement near IoT Devices

**Programme:** MSc Advanced Computer Science

**Institution:** Birmingham City University

**Completed:** 2021

The original dissertation investigated human movement near IoT devices using environmental sensor telemetry and machine-learning/data-mining methods.

The dissertation documented data exploration, preprocessing, machine-learning classification, model evaluation and comparison. The original work reported a tuned Random Forest result of 81.81% accuracy on its balanced test set.

This record is a simple reference page. The full dissertation remains the original university project document and is not rewritten here as new research.

## Separation from current research

The current research extension focuses on reliability and generalisation under temporal and device distribution changes. Those later experiments are separate from the 2021 MSc dissertation.
